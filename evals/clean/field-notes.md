# Field notes, March

We put the third sensor on the north fence after the storm took out the old mount. The new bracket is steel and should outlast the timber one. Readings from the first week look sensible, although the humidity trace drifts upward overnight and nobody has worked out why.

Sam thinks the drift comes from condensation inside the housing. If so, a vent plug would fix it. I would rather swap the sensor with the spare from the shed first, because that tells us whether the fault moves with the unit or stays with the site.

The rain gauge on the east side is still blocked with leaves. Clearing it takes ten minutes and it keeps getting forgotten, so it is now on the Monday list.

Power has been fine. The panel kept the battery above 12.4 V through four overcast days, which is better than the spec sheet suggested. We will check it again in winter, when the days are shorter and the sun sits lower behind the ridge.
